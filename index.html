黑洞物理學

黑洞結構

ISCO 最內穩定軌道 (6M)
光子球 (3M)
事件視界 (2M)
奇點

愛因斯坦場方程

Rμν - ½gμνR = (8πG/c4) Tμν

史瓦西度規是此方程在真空（T=0）、球對稱、不旋轉質量下的精確解。描述了質量如何彎曲時空。

史瓦西度規

ds² = -(1 - rs/r)c²dt² + (1 - rs/r)-1dr² + r²(dθ² + sin²θ dφ²)
rs = 2GM/c²   （史瓦西半徑）

此度規描述黑洞周圍的時空幾何。當 r → rs 時，時間膨脹趨於無窮大；光無法從事件視界內逃逸。

零測地線方程（光的路徑）

光線沿零測地線（ds²=0）傳播，由歐拉-拉格朗日方程推導：

d²r/dλ² = (r - 3M) · (dφ/dλ)²
d²φ/dλ² = -(2/r) · (dr/dλ) · (dφ/dλ)

(r−3M) 項編碼了廣義相對論的光線偏折。在 r=3M 處此項為零：光子可無限環繞（光子球）。r<3M 時所有光子都會螺旋落入黑洞。

廣義相對論修正項

aGR = -3(GM/c²) · L²/r4

相比牛頓力學的 1/r²，此額外的 1/r4 項使光線偏折角加倍，產生愛因斯坦環和正確的黑洞陰影大小。這是廣義相對論預測與牛頓重力的關鍵區別。

引力紅移
z = √(1 - rs/r)
光子在逃離重力場時損失能量。在事件視界處 z→0（無限紅移），光被完全困住。
都卜勒聚束效應
D = 1/(γ(1 - β·cosθ))
吸積盤物質以 ~0.5c 軌道速度運動，I觀測 = D4·I發射。接近側明亮偏藍，遠離側暗淡偏紅。
光子球
rph = 3M = 1.5 rs
不穩定的圓形光子軌道。經過此處附近的光子會繞行多圈，產生明亮的光子環（photon ring）。
最內穩定圓軌道 (ISCO)
risco = 6M = 3 rs
吸積盤的內邊緣。小於此半徑的物質將不可避免地落入黑洞。Kerr 黑洞的 ISCO 隨自旋縮小。
黑洞陰影
rshadow = 3√3 · M ≈ 5.2M
遠方觀測者看到的暗影表觀大小。已由事件視界望遠鏡（EHT）在 M87* 和 Sgr A* 中證實。
克爾度規（自旋）
r+ = M + √(M² - a²)
旋轉黑洞產生參考系拖曳效應，事件視界與 ISCO 縮小。極端自旋 a→M 時，ISCO→M。

RK4 數值積分

k1 = h·f(y)    k2 = h·f(y + k1/2)    k3 = h·f(y + k2/2)    k4 = h·f(y + k3)
yn+1 = yn + (k1 + 2k2 + 2k3 + k4)/6

四階龍格-庫塔法：在 GPU 片段著色器中逐像素追蹤每個光子在彎曲時空中的路徑。自適應步長確保靠近視界時的精度。

能層與彭羅斯過程

能層（Ergosphere）
rergo(θ) = M + √(M² - a²cos²θ)
克爾黑洞的事件視界之外存在一個特殊區域——能層。在能層內，時空被拖曳得如此劇烈，以至於沒有任何物體可以保持靜止。赤道面上能層最大，在自旋軸（兩極）處與事件視界重合。按 G 鍵可視化。
彭羅斯過程
EB = Ein + |EA|
1969 年由羅傑·彭羅斯提出的能量提取機制。一個粒子進入能層後分裂為二：碎片 A 沿逆行軌道墜入黑洞（帶負能量），碎片 B 帶著更多的能量逃逸。能量來源是黑洞的旋轉動能。最大效率可達 20.7%（極端克爾黑洞）。

潮汐力與義大利麵化

Ftidal = 2GM/r³ · Δr

潮汐力源自重力場的梯度：靠近黑洞的一側受到更強的引力。當物體接近奇點時，潮汐力趨於無窮大，將物體沿徑向拉伸、橫向壓縮——稱為「義大利麵化」（spaghettification）。對於恆星級黑洞（~10 M☉），義大利麵化在視界外就會發生；而超大質量黑洞（~109 M☉）的潮汐力在視界處反而較弱，穿越視界時可能「毫無感覺」。色圖：綠=溫和，黃=中等，紅=致命。

引力波

h+ ∝ a² · sin(2θ) · cos(2φ - ωt)

根據廣義相對論，加速的質量會產生時空的漣漪——引力波。本模擬展示的是旋轉黑洞的四極輻射模式：星場方向受到微小的週期性扭曲，振幅正比於自旋參數的平方（a²）。2015 年 LIGO 首次直接探測到來自雙黑洞合併的引力波，證實了愛因斯坦百年前的預言。按 W 鍵開啟。

吸積盤溫度分佈

T(r) = T0 · (risco/r)3/4 · [1 - √(risco/r)]1/4

Novikov-Thorne 薄盤模型給出吸積盤的徑向溫度分佈。峰值溫度出現在約 1.36 × risco 處（而非 ISCO 本身），對於 10 M☉ 的恆星級黑洞約為 6.3 × 106 K。模擬使用 viridis 色圖將溫度映射為顏色：深紫（冷）→ 青（中溫）→ 黃（高溫）。

同步輻射偏振

環形磁場
吸積盤中的磁場主要呈環形（toroidal）分佈，被差動旋轉拉伸纏繞。帶電粒子沿磁力線做螺旋運動時發出同步輻射。
偏振方向
同步輻射的偏振方向垂直於投影磁場方向。EHT 在 2021 年首次測量了 M87* 的偏振結構，揭示了事件視界尺度上有序的磁場幾何。模擬使用循環色相編碼偏振角。

事件視界望遠鏡 (EHT)

EHT 是一個地球尺度的射電望遠鏡陣列，利用甚長基線干涉測量（VLBI）達到約 20 微角秒的角分辨率。2019 年發布了人類史上首張黑洞影像——M87*（距地球 5500 萬光年，質量 65 億 M☉）。2022 年發布了銀河系中心 Sgr A*（距地球 2.6 萬光年，質量 400 萬 M☉）的影像。開啟 EHT 對比功能可將真實觀測影像疊加在模擬上進行視覺比較。

Black Hole Physics

Singularity

Einstein Field Equations

Rμν - ½gμνR = (8πG/c4) Tμν

The Schwarzschild metric is the exact vacuum solution (T=0) for a spherically symmetric, non-rotating mass. It describes how mass curves spacetime.

Schwarzschild Metric

ds² = -(1 - rs/r)c²dt² + (1 - rs/r)-1dr² + r²(dθ² + sin²θ dφ²)
rs = 2GM/c²   (Schwarzschild radius)

This metric describes the spacetime geometry around a black hole. As r → rs, time dilation becomes infinite; light cannot escape from within the event horizon.

Null Geodesic Equations (Light Paths)

Light rays follow null geodesics (ds²=0), derived from the Euler-Lagrange equations:

d²r/dλ² = (r - 3M) · (dφ/dλ)²
d²φ/dλ² = -(2/r) · (dr/dλ) · (dφ/dλ)

The (r−3M) term encodes GR light deflection. At r=3M it vanishes: photons orbit indefinitely (photon sphere). At r<3M all photons spiral inward.

GR Correction Term

aGR = -3(GM/c²) · L²/r4

This additional 1/r4 term (vs Newtonian 1/r²) doubles the light deflection angle, creating the Einstein ring and the correct shadow size. This is the key difference between GR and Newtonian gravity.

Gravitational Redshift
z = √(1 - rs/r)
Photons lose energy escaping gravity. At the event horizon z→0 (infinite redshift), light is fully trapped.
Doppler Beaming
D = 1/(γ(1 - β·cosθ))
Orbiting matter at ~0.5c causes Iobs = D4·Iemit. Approaching side → bright & blue, receding → dim & red.
Photon Sphere
rph = 3M = 1.5 rs
Unstable circular photon orbit. Photons passing near here loop multiple times, creating bright photon rings.
ISCO
risco = 6M = 3 rs
Innermost stable circular orbit — the disk inner edge. Matter inside inevitably falls in. Shrinks with Kerr spin.
Shadow Radius
rshadow = 3√3 · M ≈ 5.2M
Apparent size of the dark silhouette seen by a distant observer. Confirmed by EHT observations of M87* and Sgr A*.
Kerr Metric (Spin)
r+ = M + √(M² - a²)
Rotating BH: frame-dragging, smaller horizon & ISCO. At extremal spin a→M, ISCO→M.

Ergosphere & Penrose Process

Ergosphere
rergo(θ) = M + √(M² - a²cos²θ)
A region outside the event horizon of a Kerr black hole where spacetime is dragged so violently that no object can remain stationary. Largest at the equator, coincides with the horizon at the poles. Press G to visualize.
Penrose Process
EB = Ein + |EA|
Proposed by Roger Penrose in 1969. A particle enters the ergosphere and splits: fragment A falls in on a retrograde orbit (negative energy at infinity), fragment B escapes with more energy than the original. The energy source is the BH's rotational kinetic energy. Maximum efficiency: 20.7% for extremal Kerr.

Tidal Forces & Spaghettification

Ftidal = 2GM/r³ · Δr

Tidal forces arise from the gradient of the gravitational field: the near side of an object feels stronger gravity than the far side. Near the singularity, tidal forces diverge, stretching objects radially and compressing them laterally — this is called “spaghettification.” For stellar-mass BHs (~10 M☉), spaghettification occurs outside the horizon; for supermassive BHs (~109 M☉), tidal forces at the horizon are relatively mild. Color map: green = mild, yellow = moderate, red = lethal.

Gravitational Waves

h+ ∝ a² · sin(2θ) · cos(2φ - ωt)

General relativity predicts that accelerating masses produce ripples in spacetime — gravitational waves. This simulation shows the quadrupolar radiation pattern from a spinning BH: starfield directions receive a small periodic perturbation with amplitude proportional to spin squared (a²). In 2015, LIGO made the first direct detection of gravitational waves from a binary black hole merger, confirming Einstein's century-old prediction. Press W to enable.

Accretion Disk Temperature Profile

T(r) = T0 · (risco/r)3/4 · [1 - √(risco/r)]1/4

The Novikov-Thorne thin disk model gives the radial temperature distribution of the accretion disk. Peak temperature occurs at ~1.36 × risco (not at the ISCO itself), reaching ~6.3 × 106 K for a 10 M☉ stellar-mass BH. The simulation maps temperature to the viridis colormap: deep purple (cold) → teal (warm) → yellow (hot).

Synchrotron Polarization

Toroidal Magnetic Field
The magnetic field in the accretion disk is predominantly toroidal, stretched and wound by differential rotation. Charged particles spiraling along field lines emit synchrotron radiation.
Polarization Direction
Synchrotron polarization is perpendicular to the projected magnetic field. In 2021, EHT measured M87*'s polarization structure for the first time, revealing ordered magnetic field geometry at event-horizon scales. The simulation encodes polarization angle as cyclic hue.

Event Horizon Telescope (EHT)

The EHT is an Earth-sized radio telescope array using Very Long Baseline Interferometry (VLBI), achieving ~20 microarcsecond angular resolution. In 2019, it released humanity's first black hole image — M87* (55 million light-years away, 6.5 billion M☉). In 2022, it released the image of Sgr A* at the Milky Way's center (26,000 light-years away, 4 million M☉). Enable the EHT comparison overlay to superimpose real observations on the simulation.
